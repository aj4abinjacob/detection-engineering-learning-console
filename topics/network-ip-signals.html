Use IP signals as contextual routing evidence, not identity.

IP, ASN, network category, proxy/VPN likelihood, rate, and distribution gain meaning only with observation time, source, topology, and legitimate lookalikes.

Why it matters

An address is a routing locator observed by a collector at a time. It may represent a user, NAT, corporate egress, mobile carrier, cloud workload, VPN exit, proxy, or intermediary—not a stable person.

Record observation facts

Persist source IP as seen by named collector, time window, protocol, trusted-proxy status, address family, prefix where policy allows, and data source/version for enrichment. Without this, later reputation/ASN claims cannot be reproduced.

ASN and prefix

ASN identifies a routing domain announcing prefixes; it can suggest hosting/residential/mobile context but does not prove provider customer, device, country, intent, or ownership. BGP and enrichment data change, so record time/source/TTL.

Datacenter, residential, mobile

Category classifiers are probabilistic products. Datacenter can include legitimate corporate/CI/API clients; residential can include infected or shared users; mobile often has CGNAT/churn. Use category as a feature with confidence, not a blocklist label.

Proxy and VPN likelihood

Signals may include known exit infrastructure, shared behavior, hosting attributes, or header/topology context. They establish at most likelihood under source quality; privacy tools, enterprise gateways, accessibility networks, and travel are legitimate cases. Do not teach circumvention.

Rate is entity and window dependent

Define key (IP, account, session, prefix), time window, counting source, trust boundary, and thresholds. IP rate catches some volumetric behavior but aliases users behind NAT; combine account/session/path outcomes.

Distribution adds context

Measure requests across IPs, accounts, routes, ASNs, prefixes, and time. A one-account/many-IP burst differs from many-account/one-IP burst, but both can have benign explanations. Retain denominators and sampling limits.

IPv6 changes grouping assumptions

IPv6 privacy addressing rotates interface identifiers and carriers may delegate prefixes. Do not assume /128 is a stable user or apply IPv4 prefix rules unchanged; choose/document aggregation based on authorized environment evidence.

Reputation needs expiry

Store score/source/observation time/confidence/expiration and refresh policy. Reputation can be stale, biased, erroneous, or contaminated by prior shared users. Provide review/appeal and measure impact.

Safety and limits

Use documentation addresses and supplied/local sanitized corpus only; do not probe or lookup external IPs. IP/geolocation/ASN outputs are network-dependent and potentially personal data. Apply minimization, retention, and access policy.

Defensive scenarios

Datacenter checkout rate

Trigger: high IP rate. Artifact: key/window/denominator table. Observation: hosting category. Reasoning: add account/path sequence. Limit: CI/corporate API may be legitimate.

Residential reputation hit

Trigger: stale score. Artifact: score source/age. Observation: shared residential ASN. Reasoning: lower confidence. Limit: category cannot prove current abuse.

Mobile IPv6 churn

Trigger: many /128s. Artifact: prefix/time distribution. Observation: mobile topology. Reasoning: avoid naive unique-IP alert. Limit: prefix grouping also can alias users.

VPN suspicion

Trigger: exit-likelihood feed. Artifact: versioned feed result. Observation: normal account journey. Reasoning: challenge/review proportionally. Limit: privacy use is legitimate.
